公司简介 Company Profile

立足青岛、面向全球，青岛猿力觉醒创新科技有限公司是一家聚焦人工智能、前沿软件开发与全球化跨境电商的创新型科技企业。公司以技术创新为核心驱动力，深度融合数字科技与产业实际需求，专注为全球客户提供智能化系统开发、行业数字化解决方案、跨境电商全链路运营服务及专业技术咨询与技术赋能。

Based in Qingdao and serving the world, Qingdao Ape Power Awakening Innovation Technology Co., Ltd. is an innovative technology enterprise focusing on Artificial Intelligence, cutting-edge software development, and global cross-border e-commerce. With technological innovation as its core driving force, the company deeply integrates digital technology with actual industrial needs, focusing on providing global customers with intelligent system development, industrial digital solutions, full-link cross-border e-commerce operation services, and professional technical consulting and empowerment.

公司始终秉持 “技术赋能产业，创新驱动未来”的发展理念，以全球化视野、专业化能力和高标准技术体系，深耕科技应用创新与产业数字化融合。依托前沿技术积累、高效研发能力与全球化服务思维，我们为全球合作伙伴提供稳定、可靠、安全、可扩展的数字化服务与技术支撑，助力企业在智能化、数字化与全球化浪潮中实现转型升级与价值增长。

The company always adheres to the development philosophy of "Technology Empowers Industry, Innovation Drives the Future." With a global perspective, professional capabilities, and a high-standard technical system, we deeply cultivate technological application innovation and industrial digital integration. Relying on cutting-edge technology accumulation, efficient R&D capabilities, and global service thinking, we provide stable, reliable, secure, and scalable digital services and technical support for global partners, helping enterprises achieve transformation, upgrading, and value growth in the waves of intelligence, digitalization, and globalization.

面向未来，公司致力于成为区域乃至全球范围内具有核心竞争力、品牌影响力与创新驱动力的科技企业，以持续创新的技术、专业严谨的服务、开放共赢的合作模式，携手全球客户共创数字经济新时代，共筑智能化未来。

Looking to the future, the company is committed to becoming a technology enterprise with core competitiveness, brand influence, and innovation drive on a regional and even global scale. With continuous innovative technology, professional and rigorous services, and an open and win-win cooperation model, we work hand in hand with global customers to create a new era of digital economy and build an intelligent future together.
